Your Enhancement Plan

Here is how the Student Voice and Partnership Team will use your self-reflection scores to support your school's ongoing development.

What is the Enhancement Plan?

The Enhancement Plan is a practical tool designed to help your school develop and strengthen its student voice activity in line with the Student Voice Enhancement Model. It provides suggested actions, identifies priority areas, and highlights useful resources to support ongoing development.

It helps you translate your self-reflection scores into clear, forward-looking enhancements, ensuring that the insights gained from the reflection process lead to sustained development in student voice practice.

The Enhancement Plan is designed to feed into your Review and Enhancement action plan, alongside common themes identified through student feedback. The Enhancement Plan does not replace actions that come directly from feedback; instead, it complements them. Combining insights from both areas will hopefully help strengthen student voice activity at a local level.

You can use the Enhancement Plan as a working document in meetings to help keep student voice, and its ongoing development, part of regular discussions.

How to Get an Enhancement Plan (click to expand)

To receive an Enhancement Plan, your school will need to send the self-reflection scores to the team. On the Summary page, use the Download Assessment Data button, then attach the file to an email and send it to LTAcademy@cardiff.ac.uk.

Once these scores are submitted, the Student Voice and Partnership Team will:

  • Review your scores and identify your school's current maturity levels, strengths, and opportunities for development.
  • Create a tailored Enhancement Plan for your school.
  • Return the completed plan to you within 2 weeks of submission.

Once you receive your plan, you can add school-specific details such as responsibilities, timescales, student involvement, and how you will evaluate progress.

If you need support at any stage, the Student Voice and Partnership Team can be contacted at LTAcademy@cardiff.ac.uk.

Roles and Responsibilities

What the Student Voice and Partnership Team Will Do

  • Review the self-reflection scores submitted by your school for each area of the Student Voice Enhancement Model.
  • Identify your current maturity levels, strengths, and priority areas for development.
  • Produce a tailored Enhancement Plan for your school, including:
    • Student Voice activity areas
    • Priority levels
    • Suggested actions based on the next maturity level
    • Relevant resources and supporting materials
  • Provide the Enhancement Plan in the agreed template format.
  • Offer guidance and support to help your school understand and use the plan effectively.
  • Remain available for follow-up questions via LTAcademy@cardiff.ac.uk.

What Your School Will Need to Do

  • Complete and submit self-reflection scores for all areas of the Enhancement Model.
  • Review the Enhancement Plan provided by the team.
  • Add school-specific detail to the plan, including:
    • Responsibilities for each action
    • Timescales
    • How students will be engaged as partners
    • Progress updates
    • Evidence of impact
  • Use the Enhancement Plan as an active, working document in relevant meetings and discussions.
  • Share the plan with colleagues involved in student voice activity.
  • Integrate the plan into your wider Review and Enhancement planning where appropriate.
How the Enhancement Plan is Developed (click to expand)

The Enhancement Plan is developed by the Student Voice and Partnership Team based on the self-reflection scores your school provides. Once scores are submitted, the team will identify the next level of maturity within each area and select relevant developmental actions from the model.

For example, if your maturity level for Mid-Module Enhancement is Level 2, the actions listed under "Actions to be taken" will come from Level 3. If you are between levels (e.g., Level 2.5), the plan will still draw actions from Level 3 to help you continue strengthening your practice.

To support actions, the Resources section includes links to general materials that may help you develop your practice. If you need further guidance, please contact the Student Voice and Partnership Team at LTAcademy@cardiff.ac.uk, where a member of the team will be happy to offer advice.

Because student voice activity covers a wide range of activities, it may not be possible to enhance everything at once. You may find it helpful to focus on a small number of priority areas, particularly those where your maturity level is currently lower.
