Share your data with us

To receive a tailored Enhancement Plan, send your assessment data to the Student Voice and Partnership Team:

  1. Download your assessment data file using the button below.
  2. Attach the downloaded file to an email. Let us know your role and your School's priority to help tailor the bespoke plan.
  3. Send it to LTAcademy@cardiff.ac.uk — the team will return a tailored plan within 20 working days.
